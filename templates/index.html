RECOVERY CENTRE / 08

Recovery is training, too.

There is no universal schedule—adjust to your energy and how you feel.

01 / REST

Leave room to recover.

Rest days help you return feeling ready. Alternate challenging days with rest or lighter movement as it suits you.

02 / MOBILITY

Keep it gentle.

A comfortable walk or easy range-of-motion session may feel good. Nothing should require pushing through pain.

03 / LISTEN

Your signals matter.

Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or unwell. Ask a qualified health professional about ongoing symptoms.
